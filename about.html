SINCE 2019

Small beginnings.
A bigger table.

Yōótōpēa began in 2019 as a humble food concept centered around mango slushies and takoyaki.

What started as a simple idea gradually grew into something bigger — a place where people could gather around food, discover new flavors and enjoy different Asian cuisines under one roof.
